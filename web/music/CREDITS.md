## Changes and playback

Both full recordings were resampled to 44.1 kHz stereo, loudness-normalized to approximately -20 LUFS and encoded as Ogg Vorbis quality 4. No sections were intentionally removed. Runtime playback applies volume changes and a 2.5-second equal-power crossfade between songs.

Source/output hashes, exact durations, measured loudness and processing details are in `provenance.json`.
